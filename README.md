# One-O-One

My cybersecurity learning journey, started from zero and documented in the open.

The name says it: this is the "101" repo. No shortcuts, no trying to master everything at once. A little progress every day, with notes I can look back on.

## What this repo is

- A home for my study notes, written in my own words
- A daily log so progress stays visible and steady
- A record of the labs, tools and setups I build along the way

## How I keep notes

Every note follows the same short format (see [`notes/_template.md`](notes/_template.md)): what the topic is, why it matters, what I learned, and what is still confusing. Writing the confusing part down is the most useful bit.

## A note on safety

Everything here is for learning, on machines and platforms I own or am explicitly allowed to use. I only practise in my own lab or on legal training platforms.
